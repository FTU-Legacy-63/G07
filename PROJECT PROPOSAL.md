# **PROJECT PROPOSAL**

## **1\. Problem Direction**

CFA Level 1 candidates need to complete a large number of multiple-choice questions across different topics to assess and reinforce their knowledge. However, the traditional practice process often follows the same repetitive cycle:

**Read Question → Choose Answer → Check Correct/Incorrect → Move to Next Question**

Repeatedly following this process may make CFA practice monotonous and reduce learners’ willingness to continue practicing for a sufficient number of questions.

At the same time, simply knowing the total number of correct and incorrect answers may not provide enough learning value. Learners also need clear feedback about which topics they perform well in, which areas remain weak, why their answers are incorrect, and what they should practice next.

The project therefore focuses on developing a CFA Level 1 practice experience that makes repeated MCQ practice more engaging while maintaining its core learning value.

## **2\. Target User**

### **Primary Users**

CFA Level 1 candidates who are currently in the question-practice stage of their preparation.

These users:

* have already been exposed to basic CFA Level 1 concepts;  
* need to practice a large number of multiple-choice questions;  
* need to evaluate their current level of understanding;  
* need to identify weak topics;  
* need to maintain practice across multiple topics and sessions;  
* may find traditional repeated MCQ practice monotonous.

The product is therefore designed primarily as a **practice and reinforcement tool**, rather than a platform for teaching CFA Level 1 knowledge from the beginning.

## **3\. User Task**

The core user task is to practice CFA Level 1 questions, evaluate performance, identify weak areas, and decide what to practice next.

Typical flow:

1. Select a CFA topic or group of topics.  
2. Complete CFA Level 1 multiple-choice questions.  
3. Receive correctness feedback.  
4. Review explanations for incorrect answers.  
5. View overall and topic-level performance.  
6. Identify the weakest knowledge areas.  
7. Decide which topic should be practiced next.  
8. Continue another practice session.

The main decision the user should be able to make after each practice attempt is:

**“Which topic am I currently weak in, and what should I practice next?”**

## **4\. Desired User Outcome**

Users should be able to:

* reinforce CFA Level 1 knowledge through repeated question practice;  
* understand which questions they answered correctly or incorrectly;  
* understand why an incorrect answer is wrong;  
* evaluate their performance across different CFA topics;  
* identify their weakest knowledge areas;  
* determine which topics should receive additional practice;  
* remain willing to continue practicing despite the repetitive nature of CFA multiple-choice questions.

The project therefore targets two major outcomes:

**Learning Outcome:**  
Users understand what they know, what they are weak at, and what they should practice next.

**Engagement Outcome:**  
Users are willing to continue practicing despite the repetitive nature of CFA Level 1 MCQs.

## **5\. Product Statement**

**CFA Quest** is a gamified CFA Level 1 practice platform where users complete multiple-choice questions across different CFA topics, receive explanations and topic-level performance feedback, identify their weakest areas, and use supporting game mechanics to make repeated practice more engaging.

The gamification layer is intended to support the learning process rather than replace the core CFA practice activity.

## **6\. Main Output**

The main output is a practice result that provides users with information about both their overall performance and their performance across individual CFA topics.

The result may include:

* total number of questions attempted;  
* number of correct and incorrect answers;  
* **Overall Accuracy**;  
* **Topic Accuracy**;  
* strongest topics;  
* **Weakest Areas**;  
* explanation for incorrect answers;  
* recommendation on which topic should be practiced next;  
* supporting game-related rewards or progress indicators.

The primary output of the product remains **CFA learning performance**, while game scores or rewards act only as supporting engagement elements.

## **7\. Product Pattern**

**Select CFA Topic → Answer Questions → Check Correctness → Receive Explanation → Calculate Performance → Identify Weak Areas → Recommend Next Practice → Continue Practice**

## **8\. Finance and Banking Relevance**

The project directly supports the practice and evaluation of financial knowledge covered in the CFA Level 1 curriculum.

The current concept may include questions from major CFA Level 1 areas such as:

* Financial Statement Analysis;  
* Corporate Issuers / Corporate Finance;  
* Equity Investments;  
* Fixed Income;  
* Derivatives;  
* Economics;  
* Quantitative Methods;  
* Portfolio Management.

## **9\. Feasibility**

The MVP can focus on a limited number of CFA topics, a manageable question bank, automatic answer checking, explanations, and topic-level performance analysis. Gamification features should remain simple and supportive of the core CFA practice experience. 

## **10\. Revision Notes**

After reviewing the initial concept, the group clarified that:

* the project should primarily be a **CFA practice product**, not a platform that teaches CFA Level 1 from the beginning;  
* the main problem should focus on the repetitive nature of CFA MCQ practice rather than individual game features;  
* gamification should act as an **engagement layer** around the core learning loop;  
* learning performance should remain more important than game scores or rewards;  
* post-practice results should provide more information than a simple total score;  
* the product should help users identify weak topics and decide what to practice next;  
* both **engagement outcome** and **learning outcome** should eventually be evaluated.
